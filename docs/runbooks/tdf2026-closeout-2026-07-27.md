### Two false alarms caught and fixed during verification (not pipeline bugs)

1. **Stage 1-20 "regression"** — the pre-closeout baseline doc had 8 manual
   addition errors (e.g. Leopard_Trek recorded as 2960.00, correct sum
   3081.50). Independently re-summing the raw per-stage data pulled earlier
   in this session confirmed every team's stage-1-20 total is **byte-identical**
   before and after the stage-21 import. Fixed in the baseline doc and in the
   script's `BASELINE_STAGES_1_20` constant.
2. **60 "squad-membership violations"** — the first script draft iterated
   over `contracts` across **all leagues**, not just league V2. Riders under
   contract in another league (manager mode, demo league) who aren't in this
   league's Tour squad correctly earn 0 XP there — that's expected, not a
   leak. Fixed by scoping the `contracts` fetch to `LEAGUE_ID` and by only
   flagging a violation when a stored `rider_xp_daily` row actually exists for
   a non-squad-member (confirmed 0 by direct SQL across all leagues).
